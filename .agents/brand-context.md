# Brand Context

## Brand
- **Name**: Jules in Bloom
- **Category**: Wellness / somatic healing — yoga, sound healing, and community gatherings
- **Description**: A queer-affirming wellness sanctuary in Atlanta where public health
  expertise meets somatic and sacred practice.
- **Stage**: Pre-launch
- **Website**: TBD (site built; public URL not yet recorded here)

## Audience
- **Primary Audience**: Queer and trans adults in Atlanta seeking body-based healing in
  spaces that don't require them to explain or defend themselves. Skews toward people
  carrying burnout, grief, or nervous-system dysregulation, and toward those who have felt
  unwelcome or unseen in mainstream wellness spaces. Secondary: allies and wellness-curious
  Atlantans drawn by the research credibility.
- **Key Problem**: Mainstream wellness is often neither safe nor credible for them — either
  it's affirming but woo-adjacent and evidence-free, or it's clinical but not queer-affirming.
  They shouldn't have to choose between rigor and belonging.
- **Their Language**: "nervous system," "regulation," "burnout," "trauma-informed,"
  "queer-affirming," "grounding," "reclamation," "holding space," "consent-forward,"
  "all bodies," "return to my body," "somatic"

## Positioning
- **Differentiation**: Jules holds a Doctor of Public Health alongside RYT-200 and advanced
  sound certification — evidence-based practice and ancient somatic wisdom in the same
  practitioner, not one borrowing language from the other. Framed as "rooted in research,
  led by spirit." Wellness is treated as a social justice issue and healing as an act of
  reclamation, which makes the queer-affirming stance a conviction rather than a marketing
  posture.
- **Competitors**: TBD — Atlanta yoga studios, independent sound practitioners, and
  queer-affirming therapists and wellness collectives. Needs naming before positioning work.
- **Market Position**: Mid-market — priced comparably to other Atlanta studios and
  practitioners, competing on the depth and credibility of the experience rather than on
  price or exclusivity.

## Brand Personality
- **Personality Words**: grounded, reverent, scholarly, tender, unapologetically queer
- **Tone**: Warm and intimate, lowercase and unhurried, closer to poetry than to clinical
  copy — but never vague. Serious about the science, gentle in the delivery. Sacred without
  being precious; never performative or self-help-bright.
- **Voice Admires**: TBD

## Values & Mission
- **Core Values**: wellness as social justice · healing as reclamation, not repair ·
  consent-forward and trauma-informed practice · accessibility for all bodies ·
  evidence and spirit held together
- **Mission**: To create queer-affirming spaces where research meets ritual, helping people
  return to the home of their own bodies.
  Guiding line: "Healing isn't about fixing what's broken, but remembering what was always whole."

## Goals
- **Primary Goal**: Fill more sessions — book out private sessions, classes, and gatherings
  consistently.
- **Key Metrics**: Bookings per month · repeat-booking rate · email list size ·
  gathering fill rate · revenue per offering
- **Note**: Pre-launch stage and a bookings-first goal are in tension — there's no warm
  audience to fill sessions from yet. Expect the first phase of work to be audience and
  list building in service of bookings, not direct-response booking pushes.

## Offerings
- Yoga — grounding, trauma-informed, queer-affirming flows for all bodies
- Sound healing — crystal singing bowls and vibrational medicine for nervous-system quieting
- Intentional events & retreats — ceremonies, workshops, and sacred gatherings
- Research & speaking — neuro-somatic inquiry and community health research with equity at the center

Venues to date: Candler Park Sanctuary, Inman Park Studio, Blue Ridge Sanctuary & Forest,
and a virtual sanctuary over Zoom.
